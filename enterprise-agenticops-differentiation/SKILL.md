# Enterprise AgenticOps Differentiation

Use this skill to turn broad AI, network, automation, or operations experience into a sharper enterprise AgenticOps positioning story.

The goal is to make the audience quickly understand:

`This person has not just played with AI. They have delivered AI or agentic capability in a real enterprise operations environment.`

## Core Positioning

Do not position the user as an AI generalist.

Position the user as someone who understands the full path:

`traditional operations -> agentic workflows -> production adoption -> measurable operational value`

Lead with operational credibility, governed delivery, and practical outcomes.

## Workflow

1. Identify the real differentiator.
   Extract evidence that shows:
   - enterprise operating context
   - operational problem ownership
   - production delivery, not only prototype work
   - integration with trusted systems or workflows
   - governance, controls, or human approval points
   - measurable operational outcomes

2. Reframe the experience around AgenticOps maturity.
   Emphasize:
   - operationalising AI
   - embedding workflows into day-to-day support
   - enabling teams, not just building tools
   - moving from demo to production

3. Translate technical work into leadership-ready language.
   Show:
   - what operational problem existed
   - how agentic workflows were designed or delivered
   - how safety, governance, and adoption were handled
   - what changed for operations teams

4. Tailor the output to the destination.
   Common outputs:
   - LinkedIn headline or About section
   - CV profile or selected experience section
   - interview answer framework
   - networking message
   - positioning statement for applications

5. Keep the tone practical.
   Prefer evidence-led phrasing such as `delivered`, `productionised`, `embedded`, `governed`, `adopted`, and `measured`.
   Avoid hype-heavy phrasing such as `revolutionary`, `disruptive`, `guru`, or `expert in everything`.

## Writing Rules

- Distinguish enterprise delivery from AI experimentation.
- Connect AI capability to operational problems, support workflows, and business value.
- Make governance visible: approval points, auditability, access controls, safe boundaries.
- Show the systems, data sources, and teams involved when known.
- Quantify outcomes where defensible.
- Keep claims proportionate and interview-defensible.

## Default Narrative Shape

When drafting positioning copy, aim to cover:
- the operational problem
- the agentic or AI-assisted approach
- proof that it was used in a real environment
- the measurable or practical outcome
- the lesson about governance, trust, or adoption

## Output Patterns

Useful output types include:
- a core positioning statement
- 3-5 resume or LinkedIn proof points
- a short elevator pitch
- a structured interview answer
- differentiated headline or tagline options
