# Cover Letter Strategy Checklist

## Purpose

The cover letter is not a second resume.
It should explain fit, relevance, judgment, and motivation in a way the resume cannot.

## Opening

- Does the opening state fit quickly?
- Does it align to the actual role rather than generic enthusiasm?
- Does it avoid sounding templated?

## Middle

- Does it explain why the candidate's background maps to this role?
- Does it focus on 2-3 strongest reasons rather than listing everything?
- Does it translate technical work into business, service, delivery, or transformation value?

## Tone

- Direct, confident, not inflated
- No generic flattery
- No repeated resume bullets in paragraph form
- No weak “I believe I am a strong candidate because...” phrasing

## Close

- Tied back to the employer, function, or role context
- Clear and professional
- No unnecessary repetition

## Final test

After reading the letter, would a hiring manager have a clearer reason to shortlist the candidate than from the resume alone?
