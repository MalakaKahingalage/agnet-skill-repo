# Resume Strategy Checklist

Use this checklist when tailoring a resume or CV for a specific role.

## Top-of-document checks

- Does the headline sound like the target role, not just the current role?
- Does the summary reflect the employer's buying criteria?
- Are the strongest 2-4 proof points near the top?
- Are the best numbers visible in the first page?

## Relevance checks

- Is the most relevant experience expanded?
- Is the less relevant experience compressed?
- Are the bullets ordered by value to the target role?
- Are transferable strengths made explicit where direct domain match is weaker?

## Evidence checks

- Is there enough measurable proof?
- Is leadership scale visible where relevant?
- Is complexity visible: scale, risk, stakeholders, environment?
- Are outcomes framed in business or operational terms, not just technical ones?

## Language checks

- Replace generic verbs with concrete verbs.
- Remove filler and repeated claims.
- Remove low-value tool clutter from the top of the document.
- Cut claims that are hard to defend.

## Final shortlist test

If a hiring manager reads only:
- headline
- summary
- top proof points
- first half of the first role

Would they understand why the candidate fits?
