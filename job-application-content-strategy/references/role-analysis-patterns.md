# Role Analysis Patterns

Use these prompts mentally when reading a job ad.

## What is the employer really buying?

- A builder?
- A stabilizer?
- A transformer?
- A people leader?
- A product owner?
- A stakeholder translator?
- A consulting-style change agent?

## What kind of proof matters most?

- Delivery scale
- Team leadership
- Revenue or cost impact
- Operational uplift
- Risk reduction
- Adoption and change management
- Technical depth in a niche domain

## What is probably secondary?

- Long tool inventories
- Old experience that does not support the target role
- Interesting but low-relevance projects
- Broad claims without measurable outcomes

## What kind of narrative should win?

- “Already doing this”
- “Adjacent but strongly transferable”
- “Specialist depth with leadership range”
- “Operational leader with transformation proof”
- “Technical credibility plus stakeholder judgment”

## When the role is ambiguous

If the job ad mixes multiple role shapes, identify the dominant one:
- product
- programme
- consulting
- operations
- architecture
- people leadership

Then tailor primarily to that dominant shape instead of trying to serve every line equally.
